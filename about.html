About The Media School of Kenya

Equip Talent, Shape Tomorrow through world-class technical education. We are a premier practical training institution dedicated to preparing learners for thriving careers in media, communication, and digital technology.

Mission & Vision

Our Mission

To provide accessible, hands-on digital skills training that empowers individuals across Africa to build sustainable tech careers.

Our Vision

To be the premier technical training institution driving innovation and digital transformation throughout the continent.

Our History

Founded in 2020, we started with a handful of tech enthusiasts and have grown into a thriving community training thousands of students. Beginning with small weekend practical workshops in Nairobi, The Media School of Kenya expanded its facilities to include modern broadcast studios, editing suites, and computer laboratories—equipping learners with real-world skills in Broadcast, Web Design, Video Editing, Print Journalism, Engineering, Media Technology, and Mass Communication.
